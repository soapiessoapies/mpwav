Pattern

Click or tap a square to add a note, again to remove it. Drag with a mouse to paint. With a keyboard, arrows move and Enter adds or removes. Space plays and stops the loop from anywhere.

Synth

Morph

Plays the selected track. Tap or click the keys, or use your computer keys A W S E D … K (they work even with the keyboard hidden). Z and X change octave, Esc stops every note. On a focused key, arrows move and Enter plays.
