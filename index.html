Pattern

Pattern slot

Click or tap a square to add a note, again to remove it. Drag with a mouse to paint. With a keyboard, arrows move and Enter adds or removes. Space plays and stops from anywhere.

Record: the loop plays and whatever you play on the keyboard is added to this pattern, snapped to the nearest step. Each pass adds on top; Undo take removes the last recording.

Plays the selected track. Tap or click the keys, or use your computer keys A W S E D … K (they work from any tab, even with the keyboard hidden). Z and X change octave, Esc stops every note. On a focused key, arrows move and Enter plays.

Arrange

When you press Play

Each square is one bar. Tap a square to choose which pattern plays there: A, B, C, D, then empty again. Drag along a row with a mouse to repeat a pattern. With a keyboard, arrows move, Enter changes the pattern and Delete empties the bar.
