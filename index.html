Playing box

Drag a clip to move it and its right edge to stretch it (the loop repeats). Double-click or double-tap a clip to edit its notes, or an empty spot to make a new clip. Click the ruler to choose where Play starts; drag along it to set the loop. With a keyboard: arrows move between clips, Alt + arrows move a clip, Shift + arrows stretch it, Enter opens it, Delete removes it.

Clip

No clip open. Double-click a clip in the playing box (or select it and press Enter) to edit its notes. To start from nothing, press New clip.

Click or tap a square to add a note, again to remove it. Drag with a mouse to paint. With a keyboard, arrows move and Enter adds or removes. Space plays and stops from anywhere.

Record: Play runs and whatever you play on the keyboard is added to the open clip (or a new one at the cursor), snapped to the nearest step. Each pass adds on top; Undo take removes the last recording.

Note Edit

Morph

Plays the selected track. Tap or click the keys, or use your computer keys A W S E D … K (they work even with the keyboard hidden). Z and X change octave, Esc stops every note. On a focused key, arrows move and Enter plays.

Settings

Display

Fills the whole screen with no browser bars. Esc or this button again leaves it; on Windows F11 does the same.

Layout

Auto picks from the window's width. Desktop always uses the side-by-side layout; Phone always stacks everything with the keyboard pinned at the bottom.
